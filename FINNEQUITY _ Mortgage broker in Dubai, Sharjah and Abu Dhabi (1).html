Common questions

General guidance. Your bank confirms the exact terms.

Can expats and non-residents get a mortgage in Dubai?

Yes. Residents can usually borrow more than non-residents. Under UAE Central Bank rules, a first home for an expat resident can be financed up to 80% of the value (70% above AED 5 million), while non-residents are typically limited to around 50-60%.

How long can the loan term be?

Up to 25 years in most cases, and the loan usually must end before you reach the bank's maximum age (often 65 for salaried and 70 for self-employed).

What fees should I expect besides the down payment?

Dubai Land Department fee (4% of the price), mortgage registration (0.25% of the loan plus a small fixed fee), bank arrangement fee, property valuation and agent commission if you use one.

Can I get an Islamic mortgage?

Yes. Several UAE banks offer Islamic home finance as well as conventional loans, and we can compare the two side by side with real numbers.

Can self-employed buyers get approved?

Yes, although banks usually ask for business documents and longer bank statements. Some banks suit business owners better than others, which is where a broker helps.

Which documents are needed?

Passport, Emirates ID (for residents), salary certificate or business documents, recent bank statements and a UAE credit report. We send you the exact checklist after the consultation.
